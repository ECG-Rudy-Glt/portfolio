## The problem

Medical data useful to the business existed but was scattered across web pages with no usable API. In addition, an internal need for a website had neither a specification nor a market study to frame it.

## The solution

- **Medical-oriented crawler**: development of a TypeScript/Node.js crawler to automatically collect and structure medical data, following the good practices of a company codebase (Git, coding conventions).
- **Text processing**: regular expressions to parse, validate and clean the extracted text formats.
- **DOM manipulation**: client-side JavaScript/TypeScript interactions to make existing interfaces more reliable.
- **Database modelling**: following and implementing a project involving the modelling and construction of a database.
- **In-house advice**: support for the creation of a website - project study, specification, competitor analysis, advice, presentation, using an agile method.

## What it taught me

This work-study laid the concrete foundations of the profession: reading existing code before touching it, framing a need before coding (specification, competitor study), and industrialising repetitive tasks rather than redoing them by hand. A foundation I then reinvested directly at SUPINFO and in my personal projects.
