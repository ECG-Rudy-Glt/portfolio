## Background

With a scientific baccalaureate (European section) in hand, a first year of law at the University of Tours - in the middle of Covid - quickly showed that it was not the right path. A year in IT data entry followed, before a change of direction to the BTS SIO, SLAM option (ENSITECH), passed with 15.5/20. These three projects mark the start of my technical journey, kept here to show the progression from the fundamentals to my current projects.

## Projects

- **Static personal portfolio**: a first online presentation, in HTML/CSS/JS.
- **GSB project - Medical visit management**: an application to track visits by pharmaceutical sales representatives (doctor search, history, report editing).
- **Location-GSB**: a rental management application in Python/Flask with a MySQL database.

## Where it led

This foundation led directly to the end-of-year projects carried out in the SUPINFO curriculum: SUPCHAT in the 3rd year and SUPFile in the 4th year.
